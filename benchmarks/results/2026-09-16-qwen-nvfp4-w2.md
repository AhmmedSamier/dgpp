# Qwen3.8-Flash-Next NVFP4 on two Sparks (2026-09-16)

**Status: shipped recipe, mmap n-gram placement selected.**

The shipped two-node deployment uses
`nvidia/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-NVFP4` at revision
`fc694b54fb0174e0913e6adf86691ef85a4ead47`. Both ranks use FP8 dense
projections, BF16 K/V, a 262,144-token pool, four request slots, MTP depth 1,
1.5 GiB of prefix cache and the decode graph. The only A/B variable was
`engine.ngram_table`: `resident` or `mmap`.

## Placement decision

| placement | planned model memory per rank | total plan per rank | free after graph capture |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| resident | 60.34 GiB | 71.12 GiB | 17.96 GiB |
| mmap | 36.50 GiB | 47.28 GiB | 42.16 GiB |

Mapping the table saves 23.84 GiB per rank and leaves 42.16 GiB free after
graph capture. The mmap deployment became ready in 13.3 seconds from its
per-rank image cache.

Three repeated greedy runs per class and concurrency used 256 completion
tokens. Repeated prompts become prefix-cache hits. These ranges are the class
medians for request-wall aggregate throughput:

| placement | C1 | C2 | C4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| resident | 62.4–73.9 tok/s | 94.0–111.6 tok/s | 120.6–137.2 tok/s |
| mmap | 61.5–73.3 tok/s | 92.9–110.7 tok/s | 119.0–136.9 tok/s |

The current mmap placement is within 0.8–1.5% of resident at C1, 0.4–1.6% at
C2 and 0.2–3.6% at C4. The output-span C4 range for mmap is 120.1–138.9 tok/s.
All five greedy C1 transcripts match byte for byte.

The server's one-request retire records for the mmap configuration are:

| class | ms/pass | tok/pass | ms/token | tok/s | acceptance p1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| prose | 25 | 1.83 | 13.6 | 73.7 | 84% |
| code | 25 | 1.73 | 14.7 | 67.9 | 73% |
| json | 25 | 1.88 | 13.4 | 74.9 | 88% |
| math | 25 | 1.83 | 13.6 | 73.8 | 84% |
| chat | 26 | 1.59 | 16.1 | 62.1 | 60% |

Mmap adds about 1 ms to the resident run's 24–25 ms pass. Tokens per pass and
acceptance are identical in every class.

## Cold prefill

Fresh prefixes, three repeats per target length, thinking disabled:

Actual prompt lengths were 1,976 / 1,925 / 1,932 tokens near 2K,
7,714 / 7,682 / 7,609 near 8K and 30,841 / 30,952 / 30,963 near 32K.

| placement | ~2K | ~8K | ~32K |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| resident | 1.206 s, 0.624 ms/tok | 4.741 s, 0.617 ms/tok | 20.073 s, 0.648 ms/tok |
| mmap | 1.241 s, 0.642 ms/tok | 4.870 s, 0.634 ms/tok | 20.286 s, 0.656 ms/tok |

Mmap increases median prefill time by 2.9%, 2.7% and 1.1% at the three sizes.
That cost is small beside the 23.84 GiB per-rank memory saving, so mmap is the
template default. `--ngram-table resident` keeps the alternative available.

30/30 schema extraction, with no truncated answers. Both rank operation
streams matched at shutdown with digest `bd962ebdc7068b2ea7f93b712a1a375e`.
